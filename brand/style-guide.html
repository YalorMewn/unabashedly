The Brutalist Grid

This is the visual system behind the new site direction — distilled from the reference we aligned on so every screen gets built against the same rules: a raw bordered grid, one heavy display face, two accent colors, and nothing decorative that isn't also structural.

02
Typography

Three faces, three jobs. Anton carries headlines and numerals at scale — it's never used below 32px. Space Grotesk runs everything else a person reads. Space Mono is reserved for anything that behaves like data: hex codes, timestamps, measurements, tags.

05
Imagery

Photography leans architectural and high-contrast — concrete, raw structure, hard shadows. It supports the grid instead of softening it.

— Black-and-white or heavily desaturated only.
— Architectural, industrial, or material subjects over lifestyle/people shots.
— Hard-edged crops inside the grid — no rounded photo frames, no floating shadows.
— No color photography, no gradients, no lens flare / bokeh.
06
Voice & Tone

Short, declarative, second person. Statements, not questions. Every headline should survive being read out loud in one breath.

Write it like this
No more excuses.
We don't wait for motivation. We show up anyway.
Read daily. No exceptions.
Not like this
Are you ready to unlock your potential?
We're passionate about helping you succeed on your journey!
Just a friendly reminder to check in daily :)
07
Do / Don't
Do
— Thick ink rules doing structural work.
— One accent color driving each screen; the second held in reserve.
— Sharp grid, rounded only where something is clickable.
— Generous negative space around every headline.
Don't
× Gradients or drop shadows, anywhere.
× Yellow and red in the same block.
× Thin or light-weight display type.
× Decorative icons that aren't also navigation or status.
